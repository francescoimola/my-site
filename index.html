My speciality? Absolutely none. I consider myself a designer at heart, but you can often find me building asymmetrical bridges across the murky waters of marketing, community events and art.

I spend most of my time working on nevernotready projects, draging busy businesses into the present with design and words that show who they've become, not who they wanted to be years ago. My work replaces their DIY fixes and makes it clear what they're really selling.

I have some spare time coming up and I'm open for part-time work and one-off creative collaborations.

I'm from a village in central Italy. I've also lived in Liverpool and London. I now live in Folkestone, Kent. I believe in learning by trial and error. This site is my front garden.
